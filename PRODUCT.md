# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-page site (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`) served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch. Tailwind CSS is compiled locally with the Tailwind CLI and the generated CSS is committed; there is no CI build step. Swiper powers the testimonial carousel. Google Analytics (gtag) tracks outbound clicks.

## Users

- **Hiring managers and recruiters** screening Ezekiel for senior software engineering, engineering leadership, or technical product leadership roles.
- **Contract and consulting clients** evaluating him for scoped engineering or advisory work.
- **Mentees** looking for a mentor.
- **Peers and the engineering community** arriving from GitHub, Medium, or LinkedIn.

All of them are deciding, usually in a short visit, whether Ezekiel is credible and worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

Ezekiel Oladejo's personal portfolio. It exists to turn a visit into contact: an email, a LinkedIn connection, or a resume view. Success is a qualified visitor reaching out.

## Positioning

A leader who still ships. Ezekiel leads engineering teams while staying hands-on enough to deliver measurable backend and infrastructure outcomes (reliability, uptime, cost reduction, dispatch and conversion improvements), and he multiplies the engineers around him through mentorship.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from a LinkedIn profile, a recruiter's link, a GitHub or Medium profile, or a shared URL, often on mobile. The full resume lives in a linked Google Doc; the site is the summary and the proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page with anchored sections: About, Skills, Experience, Testimonials, Projects, Contact.
- Contact is via email (`iamwebwiz@gmail.com`), LinkedIn, GitHub, and Medium; there is no contact form or backend.
- Must stay deployable as plain static files on GitHub Pages.
- Outbound links carry gtag click events; keep them when editing links.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ezekiel Oladejo. Handle: iamwebwiz.
- "EO" monogram in the navigation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Headshot: `images/headshot.jpg`.
- Three real testimonials with photos (`images/testimonials/`): Israel Adura (Cloudflare), Dan Pham (Springboard mentee), Josiah Yahaya (Moniepoint). Quotes may be corrected for obvious typos only; wording and meaning must stay as given.
- Four real projects with screenshots (`images/projects/`): Shoptoph, Canvine, Scandium, RAIN Project.
- Experience entries with quantified outcomes (Yassir, Yahshud).
- Do not invent additional testimonials, clients, metrics, or employers.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: lead with outcomes, numbers, real projects, and real people vouching.
2. Every section should shorten the path to contact.
3. Fast and accessible on any device; a slow or broken portfolio undercuts an engineering claim.
4. Show leadership and hands-on depth together, never one at the expense of the other.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: labelled controls, keyboard operability, visible focus, respect for reduced-motion preferences, and text that reflows at 200% zoom.
